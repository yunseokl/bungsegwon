Support

Drop taiyaki, merge two of the same into a bigger one, and grow your little cart into a night market.

FAQ

My progress is gone. Game progress is saved only on your device. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes it.

How do I change the language? Settings (gear icon) → Language.

Do I have to watch ads? No. Ads play only when you tap an optional "Ad" button to get a bonus.
